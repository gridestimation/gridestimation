About Grid Estimations

Helping Contractors Bid Smarter, Faster and More Confidently

Grid Estimations provides construction estimating, quantity takeoff, bid support, and solar permit solutions for contractors, subcontractors, builders, developers, and solar professionals throughout the United States and Canada.

Who We Are

A dependable estimating partner for busy construction teams

Grid Estimations was created to help construction professionals prepare more bids without carrying the cost and workload of a large in-house estimating department.

Whether you are bidding a residential renovation, commercial development, industrial facility, civil project, or solar installation, our team prepares organized estimates designed to support your bidding and project-planning process.

We combine practical construction knowledge, professional estimating software, project drawings, specifications, and location-based pricing information to produce clear and usable deliverables.

  • Residential, commercial, industrial, and civil estimating
  • Complete project and individual trade takeoffs
  • Labor, material, and equipment cost breakdowns
  • Color-coded drawing markups
  • Bid support and solar permit packages
