Project Requirements

Information commonly required to begin

Available information varies by project. Send everything you currently have, and we will identify any additional requirements.

Property & Site Information

  • Complete project address
  • Recent roof or site photographs

  • Main electrical service rating

  • Utility company information

Solar Service Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Grid Estimations physically install solar systems?

Our primary service is solar estimating, system design support and permit package preparation. Physical installation should be completed by the contractor or licensed installer responsible for the project.

Do you prepare both residential and commercial solar packages?

Yes. We support residential and commercial photovoltaic projects. The exact deliverables depend on project complexity, available documents and local requirements.

Can you prepare a solar estimate without complete drawings?

We can review the information available and determine whether a preliminary estimate can be prepared. Any missing information may need to be addressed through assumptions or exclusions.

Can you revise drawings after permit comments?

Revision support can be provided based on the agreed project scope and the comments received from the reviewing authority.

What is the typical turnaround time?

Turnaround depends on project size, system complexity, document quality and the required deliverables. Standard manageable scopes may be completed within approximately 24–48 hours after receiving the required information.

Can you work with our preferred solar equipment?

Yes. Send the manufacturer names, model numbers and equipment specification sheets for the proposed modules, inverters, batteries and mounting systems.

Can you provide a sample solar permit package?

Yes. Visit our Samples page and select Solar Design & Permit Package in the sample request form.

Have a solar project ready for review?

Send us your property information, equipment specifications and available project documents. We will review the scope and provide a clear quote and estimated turnaround time.
